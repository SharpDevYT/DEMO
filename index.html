About Edu. Base

Dedicated to Excellence in Education Since 2014

Founded under the direct leadership of Aman Jain, Edu. Base has grown into one of Navin Shahdara’s most trusted coaching centers. Our institute is built on the philosophy that every student possesses unique learning styles and potential.

From foundational reading and writing skills for young learners (guided by specialized teachers like Srishti Mam) to advanced subject mastery and exam preparations for higher classes, our dedicated team works tirelessly with both high-achieving and academically challenged students.

Personalized attention for weaker students
Continuous evaluation & progress reports
Affordable, transparent fee structures
Experienced & accessible teaching staff
